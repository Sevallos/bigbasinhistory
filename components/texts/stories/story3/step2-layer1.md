This 1885 Advertisement highlights Southern Pacific Railroad's plans for a "pleasure route." As a corporation, they envisioned a lucrative railroad line and tourism venture from California's scenic environment and warm climate. This included routes toward places like Yosemite and Hotel Del Monte, showing that Southern Pacific equated nature preservation with business. Original plans for Big Basin did include a railroad line that could have extended tracks from Santa Cruz into the park, which could have well resulted in similar hotel accommodations to other parks.
