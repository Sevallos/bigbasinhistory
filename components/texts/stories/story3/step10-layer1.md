Herbert C. Jones was elected to the California State Senate in 1912, a political victory that the Sempervirens Club took great pride in since it elected one of their own into the state legislature. C. Jones was the son of Louise C. Jones, founding member of the Sempervirens Club and a president of the San Jose Woman's Club. A handful of sources remark on how Jones had accompanied his mother into Big Basin since 1900 as a young man. His fondness for Big Basin eventually culminated in his election to the state senate, where he continued to draft legislation to help preserve the state park and even to expand it, including Senate Bill 18 that successfully granted the park the funding and permission to construct the Saratoga Road.

Later in life, Herbert C. Jones would become the Sempervirens Club President and serve for over twenty years until the near-end of his life. Many of the Sempervirens Club records and photographs were donated by Jones and his family to different Santa Clara County libraries and museums, with many of them ending up in the History San Jose Collection.
