---
title: "Save the Redwoods!"
---

The founding members of the Sempervirens Club entered Big Basin in 1900 as citizens concerned for the rampant environmental degradation and destruction of the area's redwood trees. After several days of camping, hiking, and taking photographs, they left the forest as a unified organization set on preserving Big Basin. Their vision for what preserving Big Basin meant would evolve over the next few years, but in 1900 it was clear that the forest's natural beauty inspired them to act in its defense.
