---
title: Stanford University's William R. Dudley
---

In the 1890s, Professor of Botany Dudley had written in Sierra Club bulletins calling for the preservation of Big Basin, though to no avail. Its close proximity to Stanford University made it a popular destination for university-led expeditions for both research and recreation. His survey maps and testimony proved invaluable to the Sempervirens Club for the next decade for convincing the state legislature to preserve Big Basin and to open it as a public park. His support, along with other Stanford University professors, gave a sense of academic legitimacy to the Sempervirens Club's cause, as they had expert support in the preservation of California's coastal redwood trees.
