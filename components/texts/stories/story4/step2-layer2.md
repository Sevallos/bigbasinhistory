---
title: The Mother and Father of the Forest
---

For at least a century, the Sempervirens Club and visitors to Big Basin have recognized this tree as the "father of the Forest." The exact date of its naming is unclear, though it may possibly have taken place in Spring 1901 around the photograph "Christening the Big Trees." Though after the 2020 fire wooden signs and markers were destroyed, park docents are still able to point guests to both the Mother and Father of the Forest. Both trees were charred by the fire, but redwoods are especially fire resistant, and both trees still bloom green at their treetops despite being burnt at their trunks.
