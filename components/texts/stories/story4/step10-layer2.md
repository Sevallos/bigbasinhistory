---
title: Charles Kellogg
---

Environmental Activist and vaudeville actor Charles Kellogg played a significant role in the play. He played the "Spirit of Man" character that represented mankind's desire to protect sacred redwoods over the interests of greedy woodsmen and the companies they worked for. He also provided various birdcalls throughout the play. By the late 1910s, he had also combined redwood preservation with automobile interests, following similar trends set by the Save the Redwoods League. He traveled across California in a World War 1 truck with a redwood log mounted on the back, which he named his "Travel Log." Kellogg is one example of how early twentieth century preservationists worked to spread awareness of deforestation, combining art, theatrics, and new technologies to reach more audiences.
