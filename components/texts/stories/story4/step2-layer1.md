McPherson's _Santa Cruz Sentinel_ was a pivotal newspaper outlet for the Sempervirens Club to reach the local general public. McPherson and his newspaper supported the club's efforts to establish and to expand the park, hosting editorials from club members that called for the public's attention toward Big Basin.
