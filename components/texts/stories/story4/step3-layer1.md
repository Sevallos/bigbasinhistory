---
title: Carrie Stevens Walter
---

Teacher, writer, and Sempervirens Club Secretary, Carrie Stevens Walter advocated for redwood preservation up to her passing in 1907. Though the road campaign had not officially taken off until a few years later, she wrote of Big Basin's priceless value to the working class if they could enter the park. She believed in its accessibility and its use as a "breathing place [for] millions of cramped and crowded denizens of the city,"[^1] referring to the unsanitary and polluted conditions of late-19th Century cities. Walter's words in support of redwood preservation reached thousands of Californians across multiple publications, including the _Overland Monthly_ and the _San Francisco Chronicle._
