# Professor William R. Dudley of Stanford University
Written shortly before his passing in 1911, Professor Dudley stated that "the price of [Big Basin's] most attractive natural feature is eternal vigilance, and not simply the $250,000 that was paid over from their state treasury." This notion that the preservation of the park and its redwood trees required vigilant stewardship and protection guaranteed by both the State and the park's appreciators. Decades later, the Semperviren's Fund's _Big Basin_ echoed these sentiments. Andrew P. Hill's granddaughter Birdella Hill stated, "events have proved that eternal vigilance is needed to keep the forest in health and security."
