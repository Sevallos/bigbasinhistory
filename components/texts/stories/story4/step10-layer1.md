---
title: Patrons of the Play
---

In addition to a brief summary of the Sempervirens Club's actions and accomplishments since 1900, the back of the playbook also includes a list of patrons that helped support/fund the play. Many of the names listed here are continuous supporters of the Sempervirens Club over the years, including:
> James D. Phelan (Reverend Robert E. Kenna's nephew)
> Miss Bertha Coope (widow to J. F. Coope, one of the founding members of the club that brought support from the Santa Cruz Chamber of Commerce)
> William T. Jeter (and his wife Jenni Bliss)
> William H. Dool (1st park warden)
> Henry L. Middleton
> Herbert C. Jones
> Along with various local businesses
