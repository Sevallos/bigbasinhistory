# Elijah Putnam Hill
Andrew Hill's father had crossed the American Great Plains around 1852-1853, perishing from exposure just miles outside of Sacreamento where he would have sent for Andrew and his mother. He passed just months before Andrew's birth. Though he never met his father, he depicted his journey in this painting in honor of him and the potent symbol of pioneerism in early 20th Century California.

# Reverend Robert E. Kenna
On several occasion, when Kenna spoke before a crowd or even before the State Senate on behalf of the Sempervirens Club, he alluded to his own childhood experience of crossing the plains with his family. He would refer to himself as a forty-niner for the symbolic and political appeal it had among the Californian public and politicians. Certainly, in addition to honoring his father, Hill also honored the forty-niner who helped make Big Basin State Park a reality.
