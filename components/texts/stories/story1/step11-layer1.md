Tribal sovereignty and federal recognition remains a constant struggle for many Indigenous nations today. The acknowledgement and administration of ancestral tribal lands remains hotly contested in many of this country's state and national parks. Their work to preserve cultural heritage and history reflects a critical point in our understanding of sites like Big Basin.
