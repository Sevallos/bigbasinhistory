---
title: Duncan McPherson's Newspaper and Support of the Redwoods
---

McPherson's _Santa Cruz Sentinel_ was a pivotal newspaper outlet for the Sempervirens Club to reach the local general public. McPherson and his newspaper supported the club's efforts to establish and to expand the park, hosting editorials from club members that called for the public's attention toward Big Basin.[^1] It was in his newspaper that Josephine McCrackin wrote her first article in defense of the Santa Cruz redwoods. Even a century after McPherson's passing in 1921, the _Santa Cruz Sentinel_ continues to publish articles recounting Big Basin's past, such as articles from one of its columnists Ross Eric Gibson.[^2]

[^1]: Ken Berry, "Duncan McPherson: Santa Cruz Sentinel, 1839-1921," Media Museum of Northern California, https://www.norcalmediamuseum.org/?page_id=382.
[^2]: Ross Eric Gibson, "The Forest Fire that Saved the Redwoods," Santa Cruz Sentinel, https://www.santacruzsentinel.com/2020/08/30/the-forest-fire-that-saved-the-redwoods-ross-eric-gibson.
